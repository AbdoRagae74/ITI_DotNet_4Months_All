Step 1: Open ILDASM
On Windows:

Press Win + R, type ildasm, and hit Enter.

Or navigate to:
C:\Program Files (x86)\Microsoft SDKs\Windows\v10.0A\bin\NETFX 4.8 Tools\ildasm.exe
(Path may vary based on your .NET SDK version.)

Step 2: Load Your Assembly
In ILDASM, go to File → Open.

Browse to your compiled .exe or .dll (e.g., YourProgram.exe).

Click Open.
